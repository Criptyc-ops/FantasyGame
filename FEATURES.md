# Fantasy Quest: The Dragon's Curse - Game Feautures

### 1. Quest System
- 7 Quests Total: 1 Main quest + 6 Side/Bounty/Delivery quests
- Quest Types: Main, Side, Bounty, Delivery
- Progress Tracking: Visual progress bars with percentage completion
- Prerequisites: Some quests require completing others first
- Auto-completion: Objectives complete automatically as you play
- Quest Rewards: XP, Gold, Items, and Spells

### 2. XP & Leveling System
- Level 1-10+: Gain XP from combat and quest completion
- Scaling XP: Each level requires 1.5x more XP
- Stat Growth: +15 HP, +10 MP, +1 Combat Skill, +1 Magic Power per level
- Auto-heal on Level Up: Full HP/MP restore

### 3. Random Encounters
- 6 Enemy Types: Wolves, Bandits, Skeletons, Goblins, Trolls, Wraiths
- Location-based: Different enemies spawn in different areas
- Difficulty Scaling: Easy/Normal/Hard/Nightmare modes affect enemy stats
- Weighted Spawns: Different encounter rates per location
- Safe Zones: Village, Market, Castle, Tavern have no encounters

### 4. Combat System
- 5 Combat Options: Attack, Cast Spell, Use Item, Defend, Flee
- Critical Hits: Based on Luck stat (5% base + 2% per Luck)
- Elemental System: Fire, Ice, Lightning, Poison, Holy, Physical
- Weaknesses/Resistances: Enemies have specific vulnerabilities
- Special Abilities: Trolls regenerate, Wraiths drain life, Dragon breathes fire
- Loot Drops: Enemies drop crafting materials and valuables

### 5. Skills & Spells
- 6 Spells: Fireball, Ice Shard, Lightning Bolt, Healing Light, Holy Smite, Poison Dart
- Level Requirements: Spells unlock at specific levels
- Mana System: Spells cost MP, regenerate with potions or rest
- Scaling Damage: Based on Magic Power and Level

### 6. Equipment System
- Weapons: Swords, Daggers with damage bonuses
- Armor: Leather Armor with defense bonuses
- Auto-equip: First weapon/armor picked up auto-equips
- Equip/Unequip: Manual equipment management

### 7. Economy
- Gold Currency: Earn from combat and quests
- Shops: Buy items from Merchant, Wizard, Mermaid, Stablemaster
- Item Values: Common to Legendary rarity tiers
- Mount Rental: 50 gold for a horse (fast travel)

### 8. New Locations (13 total)
- Original: Village, Forest, Tower, Mountain, Lair, Cave, Market, Castle, Tavern
- New: Ancient Ruins, Abandoned Mine, Underground Lake, Royal Stables
- Danger Levels: 0-10 scale with visual indicators
- Special Features: Rest areas, Shops, Quest hubs

### 9. New Items (40+ total)
- Consumables: Healing Potions, Mana Potions, Food, Ale
- Weapons: Knight's Sword, Rusty Dagger, Crude Dagger
- Armor: Leather Armor
- Key Items: Spell Book, Crystal Shard, Dragon Scale, Crown
- Crafting: Herbs, Ores, Pelts, Essences
- Valuables: Gems, Pearls, Ancient Relics, Coins
- Tools: Rope, Torch, Lockpick, Climbing Gear

### 10. Enhanced NPCs (9 total)
- Village Elder Aldric: Gives main quest
- Archmage Zephyrus: Gives spells and side quests
- Sir Gareth the Bold: Gives weapon and bounty quest
- Trader Matthias: Shopkeeper with side quest
- Forest Hermit Graybeard: Gives permanent boosts
- Tavern Bard Lyrion: Storyteller
- Nerissa the Lake Guardian: Mermaid with water quests
- Stablemaster Hrothgar: Mount rental
- Vezareth the Ancient: Dragon boss

### 11. Death & Recovery
- No Game Over: Death sends you back to village with half HP
- Gold Penalty: Lose 20 gold on death
- Death Counter: Tracks total deaths

### 12. Adventure Journal
- Statistics Tracking: Turns, locations, enemies, potions, gold, deaths
- Map System: Shows discovered locations
- Character Sheet: Comprehensive stat display

### 13. Secret spell
- secret room in the town
- admin password access

## How to Play
1. Choose difficulty (Easy/Normal/Hard/Nightmare)
2. Use `help` for commands
3. Follow the main quest line by talking to NPCs
4. Explore dangerous areas for encounters and loot
5. Level up through combat and quest completion
6. Defeat the dragon and retrieve the Crown!

## Commands
- Movement: `north`, `south`, `east`, `west`, `mount`
- Actions: `take`, `drop`, `use`, `equip`, `unequip`, `talk`, `rest`
- Info: `inventory`, `stats`, `quests`, `journal`, `map`, `craft`, `help`
- System: `quit`, `exit`
